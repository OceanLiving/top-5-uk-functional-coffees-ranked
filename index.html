We compared the UK's most popular mushroom coffees for taste, potency, and value — here's what we found.

Mushroom coffee has gone from niche trend to one of the UK's fastest-growing wellness categories. From TikTok buzz to supermarket shelves, new blends are launching every month. But with so many brands competing for attention, how do you know which actually deliver focus, energy, and taste — and which are just overpriced instant powders? To find out, we tested the most popular mushroom coffees in the UK and ranked the top 5.

What's Wrong With Most Mushroom Coffees?

Mushroom coffee is marketed as a smarter, healthier way to start your day. But when we looked closely at the most popular brands in the UK, we found a different story. Many products rely on instant coffee powders, use fillers like maltodextrin, and contain tiny amounts of actual mushrooms.

These compromises mean you may be paying premium prices for:

  • Low mushroom content (100–300mg per serving vs 1,000mg+ needed for benefits)
  • Instant mixes that lack the richness of fresh coffee
  • Non-organic mushrooms that may carry impurities
  • Artificial flavours to mask poor quality

In short: not all mushroom coffees live up to their claims.

What to Look For in a Quality Mushroom Coffee

Not all mushroom coffees are created equal. If you want a blend that truly supports focus, energy, and wellbeing, look for these criteria:

  • Clinical-strength dosing — at least 1,000mg of mushroom extracts per serving
  • 100% fruiting bodies — no mycelium-on-grain fillers
  • Organic certification — clean, heavy-metal-free sourcing
  • Third-party lab testing — with >20% beta-glucans (the active compounds that drive real results)
  • Freshly roasted coffee — not instant powders, for true coffee flavour

How We Tested the UK's Most Popular Mushroom Coffees

To cut through the hype, our team put 12 mushroom coffee brands through a structured review process. Each product was scored out of 20 across five areas, for a total out of 100:

Cognitive Performance

Did users feel sharper, calmer, and more focused?

Energy Sustainability

Did it provide steady energy without jitters or crashes?

Ingredient Quality

Organic certification, sourcing transparency, and lab testing.

Taste & Drinkability

Did it taste like coffee, or earthy instant powder?

Value for Money

Cost per serving versus ingredient potency.

We tested each brand with a panel of professionals, athletes, and coffee lovers to see which products genuinely improved daily routines. Only five earned a place on our final list.
